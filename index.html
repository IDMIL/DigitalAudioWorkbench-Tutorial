Digital Audio Workbench 2

By Arden Butterfield, Josh Rohs, Travis J. West & Marcelo M. Wanderley with contributions by Laurent Tarabout

Copyright CIRMMT/McGill University, 2026, based on the Digital Audio Workbench

The Digital Audio Workbench 2 builds on the Digital Audio Workbench, developed at The Input Devices and Music Interaction Laboratory (IDMIL) at McGill University. This project aims to provide a workbench or playground for exploratory learing of antialiasing techniques in sampling and quantization, as well as delta-sigma modulation for analog to digital conversion.

Please follow the links below to explore different subsets of the workbench:

  • Tutorial on Sampling and Quantization
  • Tutorial on Delta-Sigma Modulation

Detailed information is avaialble in the project's README file.
